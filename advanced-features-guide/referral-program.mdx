The B2Proxy Referral Program lets you earn commission while sharing proxy services. Simply log in to your account to get your exclusive referral link, share it with friends, colleagues, or your social circle, and when they register through the link and complete an order, you'll receive a rebate reward. This article will guide you step-by-step on how to join the referral program, share your link, and earn rewards.

## I. How to Join the Referral Program?

### 1. Register as a Member

Register and complete your sign-up on the official [B2Proxy](https://www.b2proxy.com) website.

### 2. Get Your **Exclusive Referral Link/Code**

After logging in, go to Dashboard → Referral Program, then copy your unique referral link or referral code.

### 3.Invite & Earn

Friends register using your link or code and purchase any public plan (excludes custom plans).

### 4.**Earn Commissions**

Commission payout time is determined by the payment method of your friend's order:

Orders paid by **credit card**: 180 days after order completion, commission is unlocked and available for withdrawal;

Orders paid by **cryptocurrency**: 1 day after order completion, commission is unlocked and available for withdrawal;

Orders paid by **other payment methods**: commission is credited directly and available for withdrawal immediately.

<Note>
  Note: For credit card and cryptocurrency orders, commission is first credited to the commission settlement account in a pending settlement status. It can be withdrawn after the above time periods. For other payment methods, commission is credited directly without waiting for a settlement period.
</Note>

## II. **Commission Payout Time**

Commission payout time varies depending on the payment method of your friend's order:

· Orders paid by credit card: payout after 180 days from order completion;

· Orders paid by cryptocurrency: payout after 1 day from order completion.

## **Ⅲ How to Withdraw Commission?**

Go to "Referral Program" → "Withdrawal"<br />Choose withdrawal method: B2 wallet balance / cryptocurrency<br />Enter the amount and submit your request (minimum withdrawal amount is \$50)

<Note>
  Note: Withdrawals to B2 wallet require backend review; funds will arrive after approval
</Note>
